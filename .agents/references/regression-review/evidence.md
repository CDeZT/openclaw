## Attribution boundaries

- The September 13 addition is #142105 → #145478. Source attribution covers newer catalog loss and pending discovery cancellation when an older page refresh completes. The repair preserves the newer request generation; page refreshes started afterward can still retire older discovery. The original shared-request cooldown can hide the ordering on successful refreshes; a failed forced catalog request followed by a fresh picker request while other page data is pending exposes it. Recorded browser proof is on the repair parent, not a fresh installed-9.4 replay. The later automatic utility-model label and #145525 layout repair are separate and are not counted as additional support here. #145478 is absent from the 9.4 source tag.

- The September 12 additions are #97286, #141396, and #126035. Their repairs were verified against all six stable tags in the stated window.
- #97286 support is limited to skipping visibility preparation after standalone message JSON decoding reintroduced balanced hidden reasoning. #144632 also repairs streamed literal reply tags and other decoded controls; those are not additional causes attributed here. Its repair is absent from 9.4. Literal code and selected final-answer text remain valid.
- #141396 introduced a transcript read from session-row projection without ensuring every listing caller supplied the physical agent store. #143337 fixes the caller and is present in 9.4; the aggregate label remains valid response metadata.
- #126035 added cold Worker database validation under the parent-held writer. This extra scan differs from the older synchronous deletion it improved. #141434 releases the writer during read-only checks, then revalidates authority before mutation; it is present in 9.3 and 9.4. Recorded overlapping-edit progress does not establish a general deletion speedup.

- #139660 prevents new orphan update records; #143774 repairs retained state from the same #138690 cause. Count them once.
- #135179 establishes a generic installed-plugin contract regression; it does not prove the exact installed artifact behind the linked Feishu report.
- #144208 and #144715 cover recognized retired rows and a healthy missing table. Unknown or corrupt custody remains unresolved and must not be discarded. The absence of natural v1-row production in the observed stable upgrade chain limits claims about trigger prevalence.
- #144715 merged after the initial inventory cutoff. Its supplemental repair and the route repair #142798 are absent from the 9.4 source tag; merge state is not proof of a shipped repair.
- Native and platform runtime results come from the cited PR evidence, not a new binary replay in this study.
- Cursor-redelivery deletion (#140296) and Swift closure rebinding (#117904 → #134756) each lacked a second independent primary cause for their specific check and were left as evidence-only findings.
- The 8/6/3/4/4 rule counts use independent high-confidence causes, not reports or fixing PRs. Multiple fixes, duplicate reports, and repeated observations of one introducing change do not add support.
